01 / The premise

AI makes it possible to build before every decision is settled. That is the magic—and sometimes the mess. We bring experienced product judgment to the moment after the first version works, when the most useful question is no longer “Can this be built?” but “What should this become?”

02 / Our approach

Not a rebuild.
A careful shaping.

We preserve the energy and originality of what you made. Then we make deliberate choices about what deserves more attention—and what can quietly disappear.

01

Find the center

Clarify who the product is for, the change it creates, and the single experience everything else should support.

02

Shape with conviction

Keep what is distinctive. Simplify what is competing for attention. Resolve the awkward moments users would otherwise have to work around.

03

Finish what matters

Refine the interface, language, and critical flows until the product feels coherent, credible, and ready to share.

03 / The engagement

A focused collaboration with a visible before and after.

Every project is different, so we begin with the product question—not a predetermined bundle of features. A typical engagement combines editorial direction with hands-on design and implementation.

01

Read the product

We use it, study how it was made, and understand what you want it to become.

02

Set the edit

We agree on the product’s center and a short, opinionated set of changes.

03

Shape it together

We improve the experience directly, sharing decisions and progress as we go.

04

Leave it stronger

You keep an improved product, clearer story, and confident direction for what comes next.

A good fit

You have something real to react to.

  • There is a working prototype or core flow
  • You care about craft, not just more features
  • You are open to simplifying the idea
  • You want a collaborator with a point of view

Probably not a fit

You need extra hands for a fixed specification.

  • A large team to execute an existing roadmap
  • Low-cost hosting or routine maintenance
  • A guaranteed market outcome
  • More features without product trade-offs
