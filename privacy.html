Privacy

How we handle your information

Last updated August 16, 2026

Project review requests

When you request a project review, we collect the information you enter in the form, including your name, email address, project details, and your answers about the kind of help you are looking for. Netlify processes and stores the submission for us. We use it to review your request, contact you, and discuss a possible engagement.

Analytics and advertising

If you select “Allow analytics,” the Meta Pixel records visits to the site and whether a project review request was completed. Meta may use cookies or similar technologies to provide advertising measurement, attribution, and audience services. We do not use Automatic Advanced Matching, and we do not send your form answers, name, or email address to Meta through the Pixel.

You can decline analytics or change your choice using “Cookie settings” below. You can also manage advertising choices through Meta’s ad preferences and the AdChoices Canada tools.

Retention and questions

We keep project request information only as long as reasonably needed to respond, manage a possible business relationship, and meet legal or record-keeping requirements. To ask about your information or request access or deletion, use the project review form and identify your message as a privacy request.
